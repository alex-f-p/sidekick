### OpenAI

Build agents with tools, handoffs, and voice interactions.

- [Agents SDK quickstart](https://openai.github.io/openai-agents-js/guides/quickstart/)

Connect agents to your application UI or bring them into Slack and Teams.

- [CopilotKit quickstart](https://docs.copilotkit.ai/quickstart)

- [API quickstart](https://openrouter.ai/docs/quickstart)

- [Auth0 for AI Agents quickstarts](https://auth0.com/ai/docs/get-started/overview)
